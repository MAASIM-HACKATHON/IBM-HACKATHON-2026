# File Upload Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Upload Your File

Choose one of these methods:

**Method A: Click to Browse**
1. Click the **Browse** button in the upload area
2. Select your file (PDF, DOCX, TXT, etc.)
3. Click **Open**

**Method B: Drag & Drop**
1. Open your file explorer
2. Drag your file to the upload area
3. Drop it when you see the cyan highlight

**Method C: Paste Text (Traditional)**
1. Copy text from any source
2. Paste directly into the textarea
3. AI will analyze automatically

### Step 2: Review Auto-Filled Fields

After upload, the AI automatically fills:
- ✅ **Purpose**: Job application, follow-up, etc.
- ✅ **Tone**: Formal, professional, friendly
- ✅ **Job Role**: Detected from content
- ✅ **Company**: Detected from content
- ✅ **Language**: Auto-detected (26 languages)

**Tip**: Review and customize any field before generating!

### Step 3: Generate Your Email

1. Click **Generate test email**
2. Wait 2-3 seconds for AI processing
3. Review your professional email
4. Click to copy and use!

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: "File size exceeds 10MB"
**Solution**: Extract relevant text and save as smaller file, or copy/paste text directly

### Issue: "No text extracted from PDF"
**Solution**: PDF might be scanned. Copy text manually or use OCR tool first

### Issue: "File type not supported"
**Solution**: Convert to TXT, PDF, or DOCX format

### Issue: AI analysis confidence too low
**Solution**: Add more details to the file or manually fill some fields
