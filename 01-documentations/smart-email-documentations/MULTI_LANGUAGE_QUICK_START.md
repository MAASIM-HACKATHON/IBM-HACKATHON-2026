## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Minutes

### What's New?

## Quick Start

### 1️⃣ Auto-Detection (Recommended)

**Just paste your content and let AI detect the language:**

1. ✅ Keep "Auto-detect" checkbox enabled (default)
2. 📝 Paste your content into "Key message points"
3. 🤖 System automatically detects language with confidence score
4. ✨ Generate email in detected language

**Example:**
```
Paste: "Je voudrais postuler pour le poste de développeur..."
→ Detects: French (95% confidence)
→ Generates: Professional French email with cultural adaptation
```

### 2️⃣ Manual Selection

**Choose your target language manually:**

1. Click on any language flag/button
2. Popular languages shown by default
3. Click "Show all languages" for complete list (25 languages)
4. Selected language highlighted in cyan

### 3️⃣ Cultural Adaptation

**Make your email culturally appropriate:**

1. ✅ Enable "Cultural Adaptation" (recommended)
2. System automatically adjusts:
   - Formality level (formal/casual)
   - Greeting style (direct/hierarchical)
   - Communication directness
   - Email length preferences

### 4️⃣ Localized Tones (Advanced)

**For specific languages with special requirements:**

- **Japanese**: Choose Keigo (敬語) for formal business
- **Korean**: Choose Jondaemal (존댓말) for superiors
- **German**: Choose Sie-Form for business
- **French**: Choose Vouvoiement for formal

## Tips for Best Results

### ✅ DO:
- Provide at least 20-30 words for accurate detection
- Enable cultural adaptation for international emails
- Use localized tones for Asian and European languages
- Review confidence score (70%+ is reliable)
- Check alternative language suggestions if unsure

### ❌ DON'T:
- Use very short text (< 10 words) for auto-detection
- Disable cultural adaptation for formal business emails
- Ignore localized tone options for Japanese/Korean/German
- Mix multiple languages in key points (confuses detection)

## Confidence Scores

**Understanding detection confidence:**

- **90-100%**: Excellent - Very confident detection
- **70-89%**: Good - Reliable detection
- **50-69%**: Fair - Review and confirm
- **< 50%**: Low - Manually select language

## Cultural Adaptation Examples

### High Formality (Japanese, Korean, German)
- Uses honorifics and formal address
- Hierarchical greeting structure
- Detailed and polished content
- Formal closing phrases

### Moderate Formality (French, Spanish, Italian)
- Polite and respectful tone
- Warm but professional
- Moderate detail level
- Professional closing

### Neutral Formality (English, Dutch, Nordic)
- Direct and clear communication
- Professional but approachable
- Concise content
- Standard business closing

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Wrong language detected
**Solution**: Manually select the correct language from the grid

### Issue: Low confidence score
**Solution**: Add more text or use language-specific terms

### Issue: Email too formal/casual
**Solution**: Adjust localized tone or standard tone settings

### Issue: Missing cultural elements
**Solution**: Ensure "Cultural Adaptation" is enabled
