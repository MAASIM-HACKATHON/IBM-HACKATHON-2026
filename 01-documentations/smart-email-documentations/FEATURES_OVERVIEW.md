### 2. Tone Selector

Choose the perfect tone for your situation:

#### 📋 Formal
- Professional language
- Proper salutations
- Structured format
- Best for: Business communications, official requests, senior management

**Example:**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I hope this email finds you well.

I am writing to request a meeting tomorrow to discuss the project status...

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

#### 😊 Friendly
- Warm and approachable
- Maintains professionalism
- Personal touch
- Best for: Colleagues, team members, regular contacts

**Example:**
```
Hi [Recipient],

I hope you're doing well!

I'd love to catch up tomorrow to chat about the project...

Best,
[Your Name]
```

#### ⚡ Urgent
- Emphasizes time-sensitivity
- Clear action items
- Immediate attention
- Best for: Deadlines, critical issues, time-sensitive matters

**Example:**
```
Dear [Recipient],

URGENT:

I need to meet with you tomorrow regarding the project. This matter 
requires immediate attention.

Please respond at your earliest convenience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

#### 💬 Casual
- Relaxed tone
- Conversational style
- Still professional
- Best for: Informal teams, creative environments, close colleagues

**Example:**
```
Hey [Recipient],

Can we meet tomorrow to talk about the project? Let me know what 
you think.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

### 4. Shorten Email

Condense your email while keeping all key points.

**Before (85 words):**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I hope this email finds you well.

I am writing to request a meeting with you tomorrow to discuss 
the current status of our project. There are several important 
matters that require your attention and input. I believe it would 
be beneficial for us to meet in person to go over these details 
and ensure we are aligned on the next steps.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**After (45 words):**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I need to meet tomorrow to discuss the project status. Several 
important matters require your attention.

Please respond at your earliest convenience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Benefits:**
- Saves reader's time
- Gets to the point faster
- Maintains professionalism
- Keeps essential information

### 5. Expand Email

Add more details and professional language to brief messages.

**Before (25 words):**
```
Hi,

Need to discuss project tomorrow.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

**After (75 words):**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I hope this email finds you well!

I would like to schedule a meeting with you tomorrow to discuss 
the project in detail. I'd really appreciate your help with this. 
There are several aspects that would benefit from your expertise 
and input.

Please let me know your availability.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Benefits:**
- More professional appearance
- Provides context
- Shows respect for recipient
- Clearer communication
