# Smart Email Composer - User Guide

## 📖 Welcome!

Welcome to Smart Email Composer - your AI-powered assistant for writing professional emails. This guide will help you get the most out of the application.

---

## 🎯 What is Smart Email Composer?

Smart Email Composer transforms your rough messages into polished, professional emails in seconds. No more struggling with tone, grammar, or structure - just paste your message, choose a tone, and let AI do the work!

### Perfect For:
- Busy professionals who need to write emails quickly
- Non-native English speakers who want to sound professional
- Anyone who struggles with email writing
- Teams that need consistent communication style

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Open the Application
Navigate to: **http://localhost:5173**

You'll see a clean interface with two main sections:
- **Left Side**: Your input area
- **Right Side**: Generated email output

### Step 2: Enter Your Message
In the left text area, type or paste your rough message. Don't worry about:
- Grammar
- Spelling
- Structure
- Professional language

**Example:**
```
need meeting tomorrow about project
```

### Step 3: Choose a Tone
Select one of four tones based on your situation:

- **Formal** 📋 - For business, official communications
- **Friendly** 😊 - For colleagues, team members
- **Urgent** ⚡ - For time-sensitive matters
- **Casual** 💬 - For informal teams

### Step 4: Generate Email
Click the **"Generate Professional Email"** button.

Wait 1-2 seconds while AI processes your message.

### Step 5: Review and Use
Your professional email appears on the right side with:
- Proper greeting
- Well-structured body
- Professional closing
- Auto-generated subject line

## 🎨 Features Guide

### 1. Email Generation

**How to Use:**
1. Type your message in the left text area
2. Select a tone (Formal, Friendly, Urgent, or Casual)
3. Click "Generate Professional Email"
4. Review the generated email on the right

**Tips:**
- Be clear about your main point
- Include key details (names, dates, specific requests)
- Don't worry about grammar or structure
- The AI will handle the rest!

**Example Transformations:**

**Input:** "need meeting tomorrow about project"

**Formal Output:**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I hope this email finds you well.

I would like to schedule a meeting with you tomorrow to discuss 
the project. This is an important matter that requires your attention.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**Friendly Output:**
```
Hi [Recipient],

I hope you're doing well!

I'd love to catch up tomorrow to chat about the project. I'd really 
appreciate your help with this.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 3. Subject Line Generator

**How to Use:**
1. Generate an email first
2. Click "Generate Subject" button
3. Subject appears in the subject field
4. Edit if needed

**Features:**
- Automatically created from email content
- Concise (under 60 characters)
- Action-oriented
- Clear and descriptive

**Examples:**

| Email Content | Generated Subject |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Meeting request | Meeting Request: Project Discussion |
| Budget approval needed | Budget Approval Required |
| Project update | Project Status Update |
| Question about deadline | Deadline Inquiry |
| Follow-up on proposal | Proposal Follow-Up |

### 4. Shorten Email

**How to Use:**
1. Generate an email first
2. Click "Shorten" button
3. Email is condensed while keeping key points

**When to Use:**
- Email is too long
- Recipient prefers brief messages
- Quick updates needed
- Mobile-friendly communication

**Example:**

**Before (85 words):**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I hope this email finds you well.

I am writing to request a meeting with you tomorrow to discuss 
the current status of our project. There are several important 
matters that require your attention and input. I believe it would 
be beneficial for us to meet in person to go over these details.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

**After (45 words):**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I need to meet tomorrow to discuss the project status. Several 
important matters require your attention.

Please respond at your earliest convenience.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### 5. Expand Email

**How to Use:**
1. Generate an email first
2. Click "Expand" button
3. Email is enhanced with more details

**When to Use:**
- Email is too brief
- Need more context
- Formal situations
- Detailed explanations needed

**Example:**

**Before (25 words):**
```
Hi,

Need to discuss project tomorrow.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

**After (75 words):**
```
Dear [Recipient],

I hope this email finds you well!

I would like to schedule a meeting with you tomorrow to discuss 
the project in detail. I'd really appreciate your help with this. 
There are several aspects that would benefit from your expertise 
and input.

Please let me know your availability.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 6. Fix Grammar

**How to Use:**
1. Generate an email (or edit it manually)
2. Click "Fix Grammar" button
3. Grammar and spelling are corrected

**What It Fixes:**
- Capitalization
- Punctuation
- Spacing
- Sentence structure
- Common grammar errors
- Spelling mistakes

**Example:**

**Before:**
```
hi john

i need meeting tomorrow about project  can you make it?also 
need to discuss budget

thanks
```

**After:**
```
Hi John,

I need a meeting tomorrow about the project. Can you make it? 
Also, I need to discuss the budget.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

### 7. Light & Dark Mode

**How to Use:**
1. Click the sun/moon icon in the top-right corner
2. Theme switches instantly
3. Preference is saved automatically

**Light Mode** ☀️
- Clean white background
- High contrast
- Professional appearance
- Best for bright environments

**Dark Mode** 🌙
- Dark gray background
- Reduced eye strain
- Modern aesthetic
- Best for low-light environments

**Features:**
- Smooth transitions
- Persists across sessions
- Respects system preference
- Beautiful color schemes

### 8. Word Count

**Location:**
- Top-right of each text area
- Updates in real-time

**Shows:**
- Original message word count (left)
- Generated email word count (right)

**Guidelines:**
- **Short**: 50-100 words (quick updates)
- **Medium**: 100-200 words (standard emails)
- **Long**: 200+ words (detailed communications)

### 9. Copy to Clipboard

**How to Use:**
1. Generate an email
2. Click the copy icon (top-right of output area)
3. Icon changes to checkmark
4. Paste anywhere (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V)

**What's Copied:**
- Subject line (if generated)
- Full email body
- Ready to paste into any email client

**Tip:** The checkmark appears for 2 seconds to confirm the copy.

### 10. Clear All

**How to Use:**
1. Click "Clear All" button at the bottom
2. Both input and output areas are cleared
3. Start fresh with a new email

**When to Use:**
- Starting a new email
- Clearing old content
- Resetting the interface

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### Writing Effective Input

**Do:**
- ✅ Be clear about your main point
- ✅ Include key details (names, dates, requests)
- ✅ Mention the purpose of the email
- ✅ Keep it concise but informative

**Don't:**
- ❌ Worry about grammar or spelling
- ❌ Try to structure it perfectly
- ❌ Use overly formal language
- ❌ Include unnecessary details

### Choosing the Right Tone

**Consider:**
- Your relationship with the recipient
- The formality of the situation
- Company culture
- Purpose of the email
- Urgency of the matter

**Examples:**

| Situation | Recommended Tone |
|-----------|------------------|
| Email to CEO | Formal |
| Team update | Friendly |
| Deadline reminder | Urgent |
| Quick question to colleague | Casual |
| Client proposal | Formal |
| Project collaboration | Friendly |
| Emergency issue | Urgent |
| Internal chat | Casual |

### Editing Generated Emails

**Always:**
1. Replace [Recipient] with actual name
2. Replace [Your Name] with your name
3. Review for accuracy
4. Personalize if needed
5. Check specific details

**Optional:**
- Adjust tone slightly
- Add personal touches
- Include specific examples
- Modify closing

### Using Smart Tools Effectively

**Workflow:**
1. Generate initial email
2. Review the output
3. Use Shorten if too long
4. Use Expand if too brief
5. Use Fix Grammar for final polish
6. Generate Subject if needed
7. Copy and use

**Iteration:**
- Don't be afraid to regenerate
- Try different tones
- Experiment with tools
- Find what works best

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How long does it take to generate an email?
**A:** Usually 1-2 seconds. The AI processes your message instantly.

### Q: Can I edit the generated email?
**A:** Yes! Click in the output area and edit directly. You can then use "Fix Grammar" to polish your edits.

### Q: What if I don't like the generated email?
**A:** Try a different tone or regenerate. You can also edit it manually.

### Q: Can I save my emails?
**A:** Currently, emails are not saved automatically. Copy them to your email client or document. (History feature coming soon!)

### Q: Does it work offline?
**A:** No, you need an internet connection to use the AI features.

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** Yes, your emails are processed securely and not shared with third parties.

### Q: Can I use it for languages other than English?
**A:** Currently, it's optimized for English. Multi-language support is planned for future updates.

### Q: What's the maximum email length?
**A:** There's no strict limit, but emails work best under 500 words.

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Email Not Generating

**Possible Causes:**
- Empty input field
- Server connection issue
- Browser compatibility

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure you've entered text
2. Check internet connection
3. Refresh the page
4. Try a different browser

### Copy Button Not Working

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure email is generated
2. Try manual copy (Ctrl+C)
3. Check browser permissions
4. Update your browser

### Theme Not Saving

**Solutions:**
1. Check browser localStorage is enabled
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser

### Slow Performance

**Solutions:**
1. Close other browser tabs
2. Clear browser cache
3. Check internet speed
4. Restart browser
