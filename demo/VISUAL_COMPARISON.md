- ✅ Senior leadership roles

### ATS Resume Strengths

4. **Metric-Focused**: Quantified achievements stand out
5. **Structured**: Clear sections for easy parsing

### Full CV Strengths
1. **Comprehensive**: Complete career narrative
2. **Contextual**: Explains challenges and solutions
3. **Engaging**: Storytelling approach
4. **Detailed**: Shows depth of experience
5. **Professional**: Demonstrates communication skills
